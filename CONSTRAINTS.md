# Quality constraints

Sponsor baseline: chapter 13, CSP11. >=70% backend statement coverage, no capacity overflow under a 100 member burst, p95 booking under 2 seconds for 300 concurrent users. These constraints cannot be weakened by deleting tests or lowering thresholds. Security checks, restore evidence and real browser checks accompany release.

Pilot acceptance still requires five independent usability participants, all four named browsers including Safari, a WCAG 2.1 AA review, an independent OWASP review and an observed availability window. A green automated suite does not substitute for those activities.

No invented team history, approvals or usability results. Baseline money and effort figures are estimates. Disclosure of AI assistance remains in the report appendix.
