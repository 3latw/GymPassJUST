# Current pilot acceptance protocol

These are planned checks; no completed five-person study or signed acceptance is claimed.

Use five real participants with informed agreement and synthetic accounts. Each person should sign in as a student, view a marked active plan, attempt its duplicate purchase, find a class, book within the48h window and display their QR. An instructor selects the matching running class and scans. Verify active membership AND confirmed booking, then inspect the student's checked-in state. Try an active student without a booking and an inactive subscription: both must show an error without attendance changes.

Use isolated clock tests to exercise class start/end and absence after class end. Do not change the operating-system clock during the presentation. Test repeat scans, an expired/altered QR and an instructor scanning another instructor's class. Verify there is no manual student check-in control and no security/reception role option.

Test the physical camera on the intended devices and browsers; local jsQR fallback is included, but hardware trials remain pending. Record permission denial, unsupported camera, native decoder and fallback behavior. Verify QR frames remain in the browser. Test both Arabic/RTL and English/LTR, form-value retention and preference after reload.

Record participant code, task, start/end time, success, errors, comments and reviewer. Leave consent, actual findings, changes and approval signatures blank until the work occurs. Complete the full independent WCAG, browser, security, hosting and backup-access reviews before a university pilot.
